What the robot is doing

    How the modes work

    Mapping measures 360° LiDAR scans, updates free space and obstacles, selects reachable unexplored boundaries and revisits uncertain areas. It saves partial progress when stopped. Completion is assessed only for observed reachable space; unseen closed rooms cannot be certified.

    Go to position first matches LiDAR geometry to the saved map. It then plans through free space, monitors its position, adjusts the route for observed obstacles, and returns to Idle after confirming arrival. It stops if localization or movement fails.

    Confidence is a prototype indicator, not a calibrated error guarantee. LiDAR range and distance-dependent accuracy follow the D200 specification; per-reading jitter, bias and missed readings are declared simulation assumptions. The camera triangulates tracked features across multiple positions using observed floor geometry, calibrated camera height and measured motion, confirms them in another view, and adds low obstacles to an amber map layer that LiDAR cannot erase. It also checks motion consistency. This is sparse geometry, not object recognition or complete depth coverage: cables, smooth objects, reflections and unobserved spaces can still be missed.
