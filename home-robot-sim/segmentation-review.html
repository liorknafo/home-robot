Can the camera recognize the floor?

Two real segmentation models, tested on the same ten recorded camera frames. Each pair shows the camera image on the left and the model’s labels on the right. Green is the model’s floor prediction.

Offline experiment on the previous house models. Neither model controls the robot. These examples are a qualitative check, not measured obstacle-detection accuracy or proof of safe movement. The updated Blender scene needs fresh testing.

A useful integration would combine image masks with LiDAR, calibrated camera geometry and observations across time. A “floor” label alone cannot establish a clear path; an object label does not establish its distance.

The models ran locally on this Mac. Performance and deployment have not been tested on the owned Jetson Nano.
