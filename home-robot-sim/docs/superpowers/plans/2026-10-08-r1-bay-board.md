# R1 Bay Board (4-layer board under the Jetson) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Move the power & I/O board from the 50 × 178 mm 2-layer strip to one 4-layer board of at most 100 × 100 mm (nominally 74 × 100) at base level under the unchanged Jetson. Then route it, check it, package it for fabrication, integrate it into the robot CAD, harness and BOM, and re-sync the simulator.

**Architecture:**
- **CAD and board stay coupled through files.** `cad/build_robot.py` (CadQuery) writes the meshes and manifest. `pcb/extract_outline.py` derives the board outline from them. `pcb/build_pcb.py` regenerates the KiCad board from `pcb/board_spec.py` (single source of truth). `pcb/route.py` routes it with Freerouting.
- **Two-way coupling from B8 on.** Until then the coupling is one-way, CAD → pcb. From B8 the CAD also imports the routed board: a STEP plus `pcb/board_cad.json`.
- **Plan order.** B1 moves the CAD placeholders, B2–B7 build the board, B8–B10 close the loop in the CAD, the harness and the simulator.

## Global Constraints

Copied from the bay spec. Every task's requirements include this section.

**Board:**
- **Size and position:** one 4-layer, 1.6 mm PCB with a bounding box ≤ 100 × 100 mm. Nominal outline robot x −66.5..7.5, y −50..50. Board bottom z 35.4, top z 37.0.
- **Outline from the CAD:** ≥ 1.5 mm to every fixed part.
- **Shape:**
  - full width for x −66.5..−14;
  - a tongue at |y| ≤ 33.5 for x −14..7.5, between the motor notches (motor x ±12.5, |y| ≥ 35, + 1.5 mm);
  - the rear Jetson standoffs at (−33, 40) and (−33, −46) clamp the board;
  - the front Jetson standoffs (x 25) lie ahead of it.
- **Stack-up:**
  - F.Cu: signals + SMD;
  - In1: solid GND;
  - In2: power pours (VBAT_SW, VMOT, 5V, 3V3) + signals;
  - B.Cu: signals.
- **Copper:** the quote's default (JLCPCB 4-layer default: 1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner; confirm when ordering). Power paths sized from IPC-2221 at ≤ 10 °C rise. Parallel copper on F.Cu / In2 / B.Cu with via stitching counts. Heavier copper changes the price, so it needs the user's approval.

**Height zones:**
- **Under the carrier** (x ≥ −38.5): every part top, including a mated plug and its wire bend, ≤ z 54.77 − 1.5, i.e. ≤ 16.27 mm above the board top.
- **Behind the carrier** (x < −38.5): part tops ≤ lid underside − 3 mm (z 97.5).
- The old carrier-band rule (BAND_PLAN_MARGIN / BAND_PLAN_EXEMPT) is retired.

**Placement:**
- **Behind the carrier, exposed with the lid off:**
  - F1, F2, K1, C1;
  - the Pico, with its USB plug out of the left edge into the old strip-end space (x ≈ −54..−42, y 50..70), pluggable from above;
  - J1 battery, J2 rocker, J13 toggle loop, J12 arm button, J6 / J7 rear cliffs, on the rear edge.
- **Under the carrier:**
  - both DRV8874 carriers and the GY-521;
  - J4 / J5 on the motor-notch edges;
  - J10 / J11 bumpers on the side edges;
  - J8 / J9 front cliffs and J3 Jetson 5 V on the tongue's front edge;
  - U5A / U5B facing the P03;
  - the spares J14 / J15.
- **Under-carrier connectors:** each leaves its cable a path in the 17 mm gap. Either a right-angle (side-entry) housing or header, or a vertical part whose mated height plus a 90° wire bend fits the 16.27 mm zone. The cable leaves at a board edge, outward. These are plugged before the Jetson is fitted.
- **Assembly (LP3/LP4):**
  - reflow on top only;
  - ≥ 1.0 mm between SMD bodies;
  - ≥ 2.0 mm from an SMD pad to the pads of tall THT parts;
  - R13 thermal reliefs on SMD pads in zones;
  - values and polarity on silk;
  - F.Paste + stencil;
  - order: reflow SMD → inspect → iron THT → modules.
- **C1:** within 30 mm of both drivers' VIN pins.
- **Module sockets:** unmirrored.
- **Status LEDs and test pads:** behind the carrier if they fit there without breaking a rule; otherwise under it, recorded in BRINGUP.

**Rules:**
- **Unchanged:** R13 thermal reliefs; R16 Motor class with 1.0 mm clearance; R17 vias ≥ 0.5 mm from other-net pads and the 4.5 mm GND-only washer keep-out per M3 hole; LP12 one tented GND via per top-side SMD GND pad, now to In1; zero GND tracks; placement parity; route.py writes the board only on a clean result.
- **R4:** widths recomputed for the ordered copper.
- **R15:** on In1 — ≤ 1.05× for the power loops, ≤ 1.2× for the signal returns, and no In1 slot longer than 10 mm. The 40 mm B.Cu run rule is dropped.

**Mounting:**
- 4 × M3 into heat-set inserts in P_base on 2.4 mm printed PETG spacers. Start from the spike positions (−19.5, ±38), (−11.5, ±8.5) (H3/H4 moved by ruling PB4).
- The board is clamped in the two rear Jetson standoff stacks: base → 2.4 mm spacer → board → M2.5 × 20 mm standoff → Jetson. The Jetson stays at z 57.
- Printed bearing ribs on P_base sit under the rear-edge connector row.
- THT leads are trimmed to ≤ 1.4 mm.

**P03:** turned 90° in the front bay, x 9..47, |y| ≤ 31, z 33–48. Foam tape plus tie slots. Leads to U5A / U5B are about 10 mm. Re-check against the bumper riders B at full travel.

**Motor cables:** the factory 6-wire cables plug straight into J4 / J5. The W_motor harness and the motor-header joints are dropped.

**Pico GPIO:** PICO_GPIO in board_spec stays the single map. Constraints: I2C on a valid I2C pair; ADC only on GP26–28; each motor's IN1/IN2 on one PWM slice A/B. parts-and-connections.json follows it.

## Review Focus

These are inputs and conditions the spec implies but the obvious tests would miss. Each has its test in the owning task.

1. **A cable plugged into a connector under the Jetson.** The mated plug plus its wire bend must clear the carrier underside, and the cable must leave at an edge with nothing in the way. Test: B3 `test_under_carrier_connectors_leave_a_cable_path`.
2. **Plugging and unplugging the battery (J1), the rocker (J2) and the rear XH row.** The board must not flex. Every rear-edge connector sits over a bearing rib and within 35 mm of a fixing (M3 hole or standoff clamp). Test: B8 validation key `bay_rear_connectors_supported`.
3. **A bumper hit at full travel.** The turned P03 must not be struck by the bumper riders B. Test: B1 validation key `p03_clear_of_bumper_travel`.
4. **THT power pads connected to the planes through thermal spokes.** The spokes, summed over the layers each pad connects on, must carry the pad's net current at ≤ 10 °C. They must stay spokes, not solid, so the user can still iron the pad. Test: B6 `test_power_pad_spokes_carry_their_current`.
5. **The Pico USB plug, pushed in from above with the lid off and the Jetson fitted.** Its path to the left edge, and the space above it, must be clear. Tests: B3 `test_pico_usb_at_the_left_edge_with_clear_plug_path` and B8 validation key `pico_usb_insert_path_clear`.

  ```python
  """Copper stack-up of the ordered board and IPC-2221 widths (bay spec 2026-10-07 §2)."""
  STACKUP = ('F.Cu', 'In1.Cu', 'In2.Cu', 'B.Cu')
  COPPER_OZ = {'F.Cu': 1.0, 'In1.Cu': 0.5, 'In2.Cu': 0.5, 'B.Cu': 1.0}   # JLCPCB 4-layer default; confirm when ordering
  GND_PLANE_LAYER = 'In1.Cu'
  RISE_C = 10.0
  MIL = 0.0254

  def is_internal(layer):
      return layer.startswith('In')

  def ipc2221_width_mm(current_a, oz, rise_c=RISE_C, internal=False):
      """Track width (mm) that carries current_a at rise_c on copper of weight oz (IPC-2221 chart fit)."""
      k = 0.024 if internal else 0.048
      area_mil2 = (current_a / (k * rise_c ** 0.44)) ** (1 / 0.725)
      return area_mil2 / (1.378 * oz) * MIL

  def ipc2221_current_a(width_mm, oz, rise_c=RISE_C, internal=False):
      """Current (A) a track of width_mm carries at rise_c (inverse of ipc2221_width_mm)."""
      k = 0.024 if internal else 0.048
      area_mil2 = width_mm / MIL * 1.378 * oz
      return k * rise_c ** 0.44 * area_mil2 ** 0.725

  def capacity_a(widths_by_layer, rise_c=RISE_C):
      """Summed IPC current of parallel copper: {layer: width_mm} -> A."""
      return sum(ipc2221_current_a(w, COPPER_OZ[l], rise_c, is_internal(l)) for l, w in widths_by_layer.items() if w > 0)
  ```

### Task B11: Realistic IMU and odometry errors in the simulator (added 2026-10-09 at the user's request)

**Why:** the simulated gyro is ideal. `Source/HomeRobotSim/RobotPawn.cpp:437` sends the physics body's exact angular velocity: no zero-rate offset, noise, drift or scale error.
