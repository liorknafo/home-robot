# R1 bay board: ordering and assembly

The power & I/O "bay" board: 4 layers, 1.6 mm, 74 × 100 mm, routed and power-checked (bay B5 / B6). The files here are generated by
`$KPY pcb/make_fab.py` from `pcb/r1_power_io.kicad_pcb`; this page is hand-written. Nothing has been ordered.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `r1_power_io-gerbers.zip` | Upload this one zip, for the board and for the stencil. It holds the Gerbers of F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, B.Cu, F/B.Mask, F/B.Silkscreen, F.Paste (the stencil), Edge.Cuts (X2 attributes on), the Excellon drill in two files (plated `-PTH.drl`, non-plated `-NPTH.drl`): 12 files. There is no `.gbrjob` job file on purpose: it declares the size as 74.0 × 100.1 mm (the outline's line width added), and JLCPCB does not need it. |
| `r1_power_io-bom.csv` | One row per part to buy, with its MPN (board_spec.PARTS). Quantities are per board. `Fit`: `yes`, `DNP` (J14 / J15, D2), `no part` (bare test pads, holes), `plug-in` (the modules and fuses that plug into the board's parts). |
| `r1_power_io-pos.csv` | Placement of every fitted part (mm; x = u, y = −v; DNP rows and bare pads left out). A guide for placing the SMD parts on the paste. |
| `r1_power_io-1to1.pdf` | Four A4 pages at 1:1 (F.Fab with the pads sketched, F.Silkscreen, F.Cu, B.Cu, all seen from the top). Print at 100 % ("actual size") and check the 50 mm bar before laying parts on the paper. |
| `r1_power_io.step` | The board with every fitted part, for the robot CAD (B8). The parts KiCad has no model for (the J1–J3 power headers: the XT30 J1, the JST VH J2 and the Micro-Fit J3; the F1 / F2 FLR fuse holders with their fuses) are box envelopes, and so is C1 (its stock model is half the real height). ### STEP heights (for B8)

**STEP heights: max(model, PARTS h).** A part's height in the STEP is its KiCad model's, and an envelope where there is none (J1–J3, F1, F2,
and C1, whose stock model is 10 mm against the real can's up to 20 mm). `board_spec.PARTS` h is the height the zone tests use. B8 takes the
larger of the two for each part. They differ by more than 0.2 mm for these parts (heights over the board top, mm):

| Parts | STEP | PARTS h | What it means for B8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 20 (envelope, 10 × 10 mm) | 20 | Nothing: the envelope stands at PARTS h. |
| U5A, U5B | 13.8 | 12 | The model is taller: use 13.8. |
| D1 | 2.2 | 2.7 | PARTS h is the SMA's maximum body: use 2.7. |
| J6, J7, J8, J9, J10, J11, J12, J13 | 7.0 | 10 | PARTS h includes the mated XH plug; the STEP is the bare header: use 10 or model the plug. |
| U1A, U1B, U2, U3L, U3P, U4L, U4P | 8.5 | 14–15 | PARTS h includes the module plugged into the socket; the STEP is the socket only. B8 adds the modules from `reference-models/`: do not add both the module and PARTS h. |

All other parts agree within 0.2 mm (K1 is 0.1 mm taller than PARTS h; the resistors' model is 0.15 mm lower than PARTS h 0.6). The stock
models' untrimmed leads reach down to about z −1.9 (see the Files table). `pcb/tests/test_fab.py` pins this table: it fails if a model or a
PARTS h change moves a part in or out of it.

## The board order (JLCPCB)

| Option | Choose |
|---|---|
| Layers | **4 layers** |
| Size | 74 × 100 mm (≤ 100 × 100 mm: the low-price tier), outline routed (tongue and notches), one design. Check the detected size: see below |
| Quantity | **5 pcs** |
| Thickness | 1.6 mm, FR-4 |
| Stack-up | JLC04161H-7628 (the board's KiCad stack-up). No impedance control. |
| Copper | The default: **1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner** (confirm on the order page). The B6 power check assumes exactly this. Heavier copper changes the price: only with the user's OK. |
| Via covering | **Tented** (the vias are tented in the Gerbers; via-in-pad is not used) |
| Min via hole / diameter | The default 0.3 mm option: the smallest via is 0.6 / 0.3 mm |
| Surface finish | HASL (lead-free if offered at no extra cost) |
| Mask / silk colour | **Green** mask, white silk. Green has JLCPCB's smallest solder-mask dam (verify). On F.Mask the 25 GND vias beside the 0805 / SOT-23 pads have their rings inside the next pad's mask opening, so the mask over the drill is only 0.145 mm wide at the thinnest (measured in the Gerbers; tented, not opened). LIO-226 drilled those vias 0.3 mm (0.8 / 0.3 mm; at 0.4 mm the web was 0.095 mm) |
| Order number mark | Leave JLCPCB's default (a small order number on the board); removing it costs extra (verify) |
| Inner-layer order | Only if the order page asks: L2 = In1.Cu (`-In1_Cu.g1`, the solid GND plane), L3 = In2.Cu (`-In2_Cu.g2`, the power pours and signals) |
| Price | About $8 for 5 pcs at the time of the spec, plus shipping: check on the order page |

**Size check, after uploading and before paying:** check the detected size reads 74 × 100 mm (or 73.9 × 100) and the price is the ≤ 100 × 100
tier. The outline is 73.9 × 100.0 mm on the centre of its 0.1 mm Edge.Cuts line (u 0.1–74.0; the Gerber image with the line is
74.0 × 100.1 mm), and JLCPCB routes on that centre (routing tolerance ±0.2 mm; verify). The zip has no job file, which would state
100.1 mm. If the page shows 74.0 × 100.1
(the outline's line width), set the size to 74 × 100 or ask JLCPCB support; don't pay for a larger tier.

Add a **stencil** for the top side only (SMT stencil, "top", made from F.Paste in the same zip; no frame is fine for a hand-held print).
Check its price on the order page.

Design rules in the files: minimum track / space 0.15 / 0.15 mm (6 / 6 mil, the Default signal class, ruling PB17). Vias (diameter /
drill): signal 0.6 / 0.3 mm, the SMD pads' GND vias 0.8 / 0.3 mm, GND stitching, PowerTap and Motor 0.8 / 0.4 mm, Power 1.2 / 0.6 mm.
All are inside JLCPCB's standard multilayer capability (0.09 / 0.09 mm track / space; 0.3 mm via drill is the default size), so
no surcharge option is needed. The board's DRC rules carry JLCPCB's 4-layer minimums (LIO-226, `board_spec.DRC_RULES`): annular
ring ≥ 0.13 mm, plated hole ≥ 0.3 mm, non-plated ≥ 0.5 mm, hole to hole ≥ 0.5 mm, silk lines and text strokes ≥ 0.12 mm, silk
≥ 0.1 mm off the mask openings, and copper to the edge ≥ 0.5 mm (KiCad's default, kept: JLCPCB needs 0.3); the board is DRC-clean
against them. The smallest drill is
0.3 mm; the NPTH holes are three Ø3.0 mm (the clamp holes H5 / H6 and J3's Micro-Fit peg) and the two Ø2.4 mm FLR locating holes.

## Parts and mechanical items

Buy from the BOM (`r1_power_io-bom.csv`; MPNs marked "verify before ordering" were quoted from memory: check each one; B9 lists them, with the other from-memory figures, in `parts-and-connections.json` `verify`). Notes:

- **Regulator: no D24V50F5.** Do not buy the Pololu D24V50F5 (2851): the user's P03 module is the 5 V regulator (U5A in, U5B out).
- **FLR holder on a 1.6 mm board.** F1 / F2 are Littelfuse FLR 178.6165.0001 holders. The KiCad footprint gives "PCB thickness up to
  1.5 mm" for their snap lock; this board is 1.6 mm. Press each holder flat while soldering all 8 pins; the solder holds it. Try the fit on
  the first board.
- **Fuses:** ATO blade, 7.5 A (F1, main) and 5 A (F2, motor branch).
- **Power connectors: three families that cannot be cross-plugged** (ruling PB30). No lead fits a wrong header; tag the leads anyway
  ("J1 BATT", "J2 ROCKER", "J3 JETSON 5V").
  - **J1 (battery):** AMASS XT30UPB-M, the vertical XT30 PCB male, 15 A continuous (verify before ordering). It mates an AMASS
    XT30U-F (the female, verify before ordering) on the battery lead W01: Tamiya plug → 18 AWG silicone → XT30U-F.
  - **J2 (rocker):** JST B2P-VH, unchanged. It mates a JST VHR-2N housing on the rocker lead W02.
  - **J3 (Jetson 5 V):** Molex 43650-0200, the Micro-Fit 3.0 2-circuit right-angle through-hole header with a plastic locating peg
    (verify before ordering). It mates a Molex 43645-0200 receptacle with two 43030-series female crimp terminals for 18 AWG on the
    Jetson lead W04 (verify the exact crimp part number before ordering). Rating: 8.5 A per circuit for 2 circuits on 18 AWG (Molex
    derating table, quoted from memory: verify), against the Jetson's 4.5 A.
- **Pico:** the user's own (E02), not bought. If it has no headers (SC0915 comes bare), solder the two 1 × 20 male header strips on
  first, before it goes into the U1A / U1B sockets.
- **Pico cable:** an up-angled micro-USB (micro-B, 90° up) plug. The Pico's receptacle faces the board's left edge; the plug goes in from
  above with the lid off, so the cable must rise straight up.
- **P03:** the user's module, on 1 mm double-sided foam tape in the front bay plus a cable tie: measure it first (the CAD assumes a
  62 × 38 × 14 mm envelope), then set 5.1 V on the bench under load (6.0 V in, 4.5 A out: BRINGUP.md section 5) before it is wired
  to U5A / U5B.
- **J14 / J15 are DNP:** spare pad rows, placed and netted, not fitted (ruling PB8). A bare 2.54 mm header may be fitted later
  (≤ 8.5 mm tall).
  J15 is now used by the camera LED (task N, LIO-134): the LED lead's three wires are soldered into its holes 3, 5 and 6 as an
  assembly step, no header (BRINGUP.md section 10 item 6). The board files do not change.
- **D2 is DNP** (LIO-226): an SS14 from the board's 5V to the Pico's VSYS, placed and netted, not fitted (its BOM row says DNP; it is
  left out of the pos file). Fit it only if the robot must run the Pico and its sensors without the Jetson's USB (BRINGUP.md
  section 4 item 2). It sits inside DRV_R's socket rows.
- **Resistor values (LIO-226):** R19 / R20 (I2C pull-ups) are 10 kΩ; R24 / R25 (4.7 kΩ) with C4 / C5 (100 nF) are the CS filters by
  the drivers; R26 (10 kΩ) is the ARM pull-up.
- **C3** is Panasonic EEE-FK1C101P (100 µF 16 V, 6.3 × 5.8 mm SMD can) at DRV_L's VIN, under the IMU module. It is reflowed with the
  other SMD parts.
- **Mechanical:** M2.5 × 20 mm nylon F-F standoffs ×2 (the rear Jetson standoffs that clamp the board), M2.5 × 12 countersunk screws ×2
  (from under P_base through the clamp spacers and the board), M3 heat-set inserts ×4 in P_base, M3 × 8 pan-head screws ×4, and the
  printed spacers (four 2.4 mm PETG spacers under the M3 holes, two 2.4 mm clamp spacers). The two bearing ribs under the rear connector
  row are separate printed bars, not part of P_base: they are bonded with epoxy into 0.6 mm pockets in the P_base top.

## Assembly

**J1 (battery, the XT30UPB-M): pin 1 = GND, pin 2 = BAT+ (silk "−" / "+" beside each pin, ruling PB20). The battery lead's XT30U-F must
match: GND in cavity 1 (its "−" contact, black wire), BAT+ in cavity 2 (its "+" contact, red wire). A reversed battery lead is the
costliest assembly mistake on this board (there is no reverse-polarity protection): check it with a meter before the first plug-in.**
The XT30 housing mates only one way round, but a lead with its two wires soldered the wrong way round still plugs in: do the meter check
(BRINGUP.md section 0).

Order: **reflow SMD → inspect → iron THT → modules.**

1. **Reflow SMD, top side only.** Tape the board down, print paste through the stencil, place C2–C5, D1, Q1–Q5, LED1–LED3 and R1–R26
   (positions in the pos file, polarity on the silk: the LED and D1 cathode marks, the SOT-23 pin 1, C3 "+"), and reflow on a hot plate
   or in an oven. The test pads TP1–TP5 stay bare copper, and D2 stays empty (DNP).
2. **Inspect.** Look for bridges on the SOT-23 and 0805 parts and check the polarity marks. Check the GND 0805 / SOT-23 joints for solder
   wicking into the tented vias beside them (the mask web there is about 0.15 mm). With a meter: no short from GND to BAT+,
   VBAT_SW, VMOT, 5V or 3V3.
3. **Iron THT**, lowest first: the sockets (U1A / U1B, U2, U3L / U3P, U4L / U4P), the connectors (the XH headers, the VH J2, the XT30
   J1 and the Micro-Fit J3, its plastic peg through its hole), J4 / J5, U5A / U5B, the FLR holders, K1, C1 ("+" on silk). The power pads keep thermal-relief spokes (ruling R13), so a large tip solders them. **Trim every lead
   to ≤ 1.4 mm** under the board: it sits on 2.4 mm spacers and printed ribs.
4. **Modules.** Plug in the Pico first (USB toward the left edge) and check it on USB. Set the P03 on the bench before it is wired to
   U5A / U5B (pin 1 is "+" on both terminals: the screw nearer the robot's centre line). Then plug in the two DRV8874 carriers and the
   GY-521, each as the F.Fab outline in the PDF shows. BRINGUP.md sections 4–5 give the details.
5. **Under-carrier cables.** Plug them all before the Jetson is fitted: once it is on, none of them can be reached.
   - J4 / J5: the motors connect through short C18 pigtails (a right-angle female socket on J4 / J5, plugged on at the bench) and inline
     joints to the motors' factory cables (ruling PB25), not by plugging the factory cables straight onto the headers.
   - J8–J11 and the P03 leads at U5A / U5B.

   J3 (Jetson 5 V) is not under the carrier: it is in the rear band, behind the carrier, reachable with the lid off.

## Verify before ordering

Facts not in the repo, quoted from memory: check each one before ordering (the BOM items again in B9).

- [ ] **JLCPCB copper:** the 4-layer default is still 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner.
- [ ] **JLCPCB hole-wall copper** (via barrel plating): about 18 µm minimum, 20 µm average (IPC-6012 class 2). The B6 via model uses
      20 µm (`copper.VIA_PLATING_MM`). Recomputed at 18 µm (B7), only one pad is via-limited: U3P.2 (DRV_L GND) carries 3.11 A against
      2.1 A (+48 %; 3.25 A at 20 µm). The worst tap via carries 87 mA against 0.87 A, and C3's GND via (0.3 mm drill since LIO-226) 0.10 A
      against 0.71 A (0.20 A with fast decay). Every margin holds; no copper change.
- [ ] **Price and options:** about $8 for 5 pcs (4 layers, ≤ 100 × 100 mm), the stencil price, stack-up JLC04161H-7628 available,
      the 0.3 mm via drill at no extra cost, the order-number mark free at its default.
- [ ] **Size and mask:** JLCPCB routes on the Edge.Cuts line centre (routing tolerance ±0.2 mm), so the order page reads 74 × 100 mm (the
      size check above); green has the smallest solder-mask dam (the 0.145 mm web over the GND vias).
- [ ] **Silk text:** the silk labels are 0.8 mm high with a 0.15 mm stroke. JLCPCB recommends ≥ 1.0 mm characters with ≥ 0.153 mm lines. They
      print, perhaps less crisply; 1.0 mm labels do not fit round the parts (B7). The stock footprint outlines are 0.12 mm wide (also
      under the 0.153 mm), and those of J6, J7, J10–J13 and K1 sit 0.09–0.10 mm from the board edge: they may be clipped there.
- [ ] **P03 at the low-battery stop** (also on LIO-105's list): the HW-316E's XL4015 datasheet gives 8–36 V input (the seller says
      4–38 V). `board_spec` assumes 4.71 A out (the Jetson's 4.5 A + the 5V taps) from 6.0 V at 85 % (P03_VIN_MIN, P03_EFF; see "P03 efficiency" below); as a non-synchronous buck with a sense shunt
      it needs about 0.6–0.8 V of headroom at 4.5 A, and motor starts sag the pack 0.3–0.5 V more. Bench test: 6.0 V in, 4.5 A out
      (BRINGUP.md section 5). If it fails, the firmware stop rises to the lowest passing input + 0.5 V, and P03_VIN_MIN with it.
- [ ] **5 V at the Jetson** (also on LIO-105's list): about 65 mΩ from the P03 to the barrel plug (board copper, W04 18 AWG, Micro-Fit,
      barrel jack), about 0.3 V at 4.5 A (an estimate). The plug must read ≥ 5.0 V under the Jetson's load with U5B ≤ 5.25 V
      (`board_spec.V5_MAX`; BRINGUP.md section 7 step 3).
- [ ] **DRV8874** (TI SLVSF66; Pololu 4035 carrier): R_DS(on) high + low side 200 mΩ typical at 25 °C, about ×1.5 at 150 °C; thermal
      shutdown about 165 °C; R_θJA about 40 °C/W on a JEDEC 4-layer board, and 60–80 °C/W assumed for the 2-layer carrier. With these,
      T_J reaches 88–136 °C at 2.1 A continuous, so firmware limits each channel to 1.8 A continuous (BRINGUP-NOTES.md). CS 1.1 V/A.
- [ ] **C3 Panasonic EEE-FK1C101P** (FK): ESR 0.44 Ω at 100 kHz, ripple rating 0.16 A at 105 °C, height 5.8 ± 0.3 mm (must stay
      ≤ 7.5 mm under the IMU module). Its ripple share is 0.10 A at 20 kHz PWM with C1 fitted and slow decay (about 0.20 A with fast
      decay, which the firmware must not use: BRINGUP-NOTES.md). The 0.16 A is the 100 kHz rating, not derated to 20 kHz: check the
      datasheet's frequency multiplier.
- [ ] **Fuse hold current:** an ATO fuse carries 110 % of its rating indefinitely (Littelfuse ATO: 110 % for 100 h minimum, verify).
      The trunks are sized for 7.5 A (F1) and 5 A (F2) at 10 °C rise; at 110 % (8.25 A / 5.5 A) their rise is about 12 °C
      (IPC-2221: ΔT ∝ I^(1 / 0.44)).
- [ ] **P03 efficiency:** the power check assumes 85 % at 6.0 V in, 5 V × 4.71 A out (the Jetson's 4.5 A + 0.21 A of 5V taps): 4.62 A
      through U5A. An XL4015 buck near dropout may do worse: measure its input current at the bench under load.
- [ ] **The power model's remaining optimism:** the DRV8874 continuous limit (`power_check.drv8874_current_for_tj`, 1.8 A) uses the
      typical R_DS(on), not the datasheet maximum, and an assumed 40 °C under the carrier (measured in the lid-on test, BRINGUP.md); the
      via barrels use 20 µm average plating against an 18 µm minimum (above). Copper on stacked layers is rated on its combined
      cross-section (`copper.capacity_a`), but the battery GND pin's ring cut (`power_check.pin_ring_cut`) still sums its layers.
- [ ] **C1 Panasonic EEU-FR1E471** (FR, 470 µF 25 V): ESR about 0.04 Ω; 10 mm diameter, 5 mm lead pitch, ≤ 20 mm tall (the STEP envelope
      is 20 mm; the real can is about 16–20 mm).
- [ ] **Omron G5LE-1 DC5** relay: coil 63 Ω, 79.4 mA at 5 V; contacts rated for the 5 A DC motor branch; pinout as the KiCad footprint.
- [ ] **Motor encoder** (Pololu 4867, 25D 48 CPR): Hall sensors at most 10 mA each, from 5 V.
- [ ] **ToF sensors** (Pololu 2490, VL53L0X): about 40 mA peak each while ranging (ST: 19 mA average), from 3V3.
- [ ] **Pico 3V3 regulator:** 300 mA for external loads (the 3V3 rail: IMU, ToF sensors, pull-ups).
- [ ] **I2C pull-ups on the modules:** the GY-521's 2.2 kΩ and the Pololu 2490 carriers' 10 kΩ (from memory); with R19 / R20 at 10 kΩ
      the bus is about 1.05 kΩ. Measure it at bring-up (BRINGUP.md section 4 item 4).
- [ ] **JLCPCB 4-layer minimums** as the DRC rules carry them (annular ring 0.13 mm, plated hole 0.3 mm, non-plated 0.5 mm, hole to hole
      0.5 mm, copper to edge 0.3 mm, silk line 0.12 mm, silk to mask 0.1 mm): check them against JLCPCB's capability page.
- [ ] **LED forward voltage:** at least 1.8 V (the lamp currents assume it).
- [ ] **Power connectors J1 / J3 and their mates:** AMASS XT30UPB-M (J1, vertical PCB male, 15 A continuous; pin 1 = its "−" pad) and
      the XT30U-F on W01; Molex 43650-0200 (J3, Micro-Fit 3.0 right-angle header with peg), the 43645-0200 receptacle and the exact
      43030-series crimp for 18 AWG on W04; the Micro-Fit 3.0 rating of 8.5 A per circuit for 2 circuits on 18 AWG (Molex derating
      table) against 4.5 A.
- [ ] **Envelope heights** (STEP for B8): JST B2P-VH header (J2) about 10 mm; AMASS XT30UPB-M (J1) about 11 mm; Molex 43650-0200 (J3)
      about 10 mm; FLR housing 17.5 mm with the ATO fuse body (19.1 × 5.1 mm) up to 30 mm; C1 20 mm.
- [ ] **The BOM MPNs marked "verify before ordering":** the AMASS J1 header, the Molex J3 header, the Sullins sockets (U1A / U1B, U2, U3L / U3P, U4L / U4P), the Amphenol J4 / J5 headers, the
      Phoenix U5A / U5B terminals, the Vishay D1 / D2, the Samsung C2 / C4 / C5, the Panasonic C3, the Lite-On LEDs, the Yageo R1–R26 and the Littelfuse
      ATO fuses.
