HEADERS - two rows of 7 plated holes, 0.1 in (2.54 mm) pitch                                          [S] [D] [T]
 Row L = the LEFT row of the top view [T]; schematic header with VM on pin 1 [S]:
   1 VM   2 GND   3 EN/IN1   4 PH/IN2   5 PMODE   6 SLEEP (active-high enable, nSLEEP)   7 VREF
 Row P = the RIGHT row of the top view [T]; schematic header with VIN on pin 1 [S]:
   1 VIN  2 GND   3 OUT1     4 OUT2     5 IMODE   6 FAULT (nFAULT, open drain)           7 CS
 Pin 1 of both rows is at the same end of the board (VM and VIN are side by side) [T] [D iso view].
 Pin 2 (GND) of each row has a square pad in [T]; there are TWO GND pins, one per row (page: "ground connection points
 for the motor and logic supplies").
 Hole geometry [D]: 14 holes, drill dia 1.02 mm; pitch 2.54 mm; ROW SPACING 12.70 mm (0.5 in); first/last hole 1.27 mm
 from the board edge.
 Outline [D]: 15.2 mm (0.6 in, across the rows) x 17.8 mm (0.7 in, along the rows). Component height above the board
 1.8 mm, board 1.02 mm thick. The carrier ships with breakaway 0.1 in male headers (page): with a 8.5 mm female socket
 and the 2.54 mm header body under the carrier the carrier top sits about 14 mm above our board.
 Plugging into two 1x07 female sockets: with both sockets at rotation 0 in KiCad's frame (x right, y down) and pins
 running +y, the VM row socket is at x0 and the VIN row socket at x0 + 12.70 mm, same y0 (carrier component side up,
 pin 1 toward -y). Rotating the pair by 180 degrees (pin 1 toward +y) is the other valid way to mount it.

PIN FUNCTIONS AND CARRIER DEFAULTS                                                                        [P] [S]
 VIN    4.5-37 V supply input (reverse-voltage protection MOSFET Q1 on the carrier).
 VM     supply AFTER the reverse-protection MOSFET; an output for other circuits. NOT connected on our board.
 EN/IN1, PH/IN2   control inputs; default LOW (carrier pull-downs). IN/IN mode: IN1/IN2 drive the two half bridges.
 PMODE  mode select, state latched when the driver is enabled via SLEEP. LOW = PHASE/ENABLE, HIGH = PWM (IN/IN),
        floating = independent half-bridge. We use IN/IN (same as the MDD3A scheme) so PMODE is tied HIGH = 3V3.
 SLEEP  low = sleep; default LOW. Driven by Pico GP7 (board_spec.PICO_GPIO['DRV_SLEEP'], both carriers, R17 100 k pull-down on our board).
 VREF   current-limit reference. The carrier pulls VREF up to SLEEP through 10 k (R2) [S], R5 to GND not populated.
        So VREF follows the voltage on SLEEP. Left unconnected on our board.
 IMODE  4-state input for current regulation / over-current mode. The carrier pulls IMODE to GND through 20 k (R4) [S]:
        cycle-by-cycle current chopping. Left unconnected on our board (keeps the carrier default).
 FAULT  open-drain, active low, default floating: needs the pull-up (R18, 10 k to 3V3).
 CS     current-sense output, loaded on the carrier by 2.49 k to GND (R3) [S]. No external resistor needed.
 Ratings [P]: 4.5-37 V, 2.1 A continuous, default peak-current limit about 4.4 A (with 5 V on SLEEP).

DECISIONS FOR THE UNNAMED / EXTRA PINS (board_spec.DRV_EXTRA_GND, DRV_UNUSED)
 Row L pin 2 (second GND)  -> GND  (DRV_EXTRA_GND)
 Row L pin 1 (VM)          -> NC   (output of the carrier's own protected rail; VIN already carries VMOT)
 Row L pin 7 (VREF)        -> NC   (carrier default: tracks SLEEP)
 Row P pin 5 (IMODE)       -> NC   (carrier default: pulled to GND, cycle-by-cycle)
 PMODE (row L pin 5)       -> 3V3  (PWM / IN-IN mode)

CURRENT-SENSE SCALE AND THE 3.3 V ADC LIMIT
 [P] "approximately 1.1 V/A" (DRV8874; 2.5 V/A is the DRV8876 variant), with the 2.49 k carrier resistor R3 on CS [S].
 [P] default current limit "approximately 4.4 A ... a lower logic voltage on SLEEP decreases the current limit
     proportionally" => limit = 4.4 A x V(SLEEP)/5 V.
 With GP7 (SLEEP) high (3.3 V), VREF = 3.3 V:  limit = 4.4 x 3.3/5 = about 2.9 A  (3.3 V / 1.1 V/A = 3.0 A).
 CS voltage at that limit = 2.9 A x 1.1 V/A = about 3.2 V, i.e. CS at the limit equals VREF = V(SLEEP) = 3.3 V:
 it stays within the Pico ADC range (0-3.3 V) but with essentially no margin, and CS is not limited to VREF during
 over-current transients before the chopper reacts. Stall current of the motors is expected below 2.1 A (spec section 10).
 The CS output also reads full scale (3.3 V) = about 3.0 A, so firmware current limits above 2.9 A cannot be seen.
 Concern recorded in the Task 2 report: Pico pads tolerate IOVDD + 0.3 V only.
