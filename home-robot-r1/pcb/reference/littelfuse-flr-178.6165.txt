DATASHEET FACTS
 Leaf-spring contacts, soldering terminals "4 pins each" (8 pins in all), copper alloy, silver plated; thermoplastic UL 94 V-0.
 Plan 20 x 6 mm; body 17.5 mm above the board (plus the fuse body, about 12 mm, above the housing: board_spec uses h = 30);
 pins 3 mm below the housing; holes 1.4 +/-0.1 mm; pad grid 3.5 mm x 2.5 mm per contact, contacts 5.8 mm apart;
 one 2.4 mm locating hole. The KiCad footprint pads: four pads numbered 1 at x = 0 and 3.5, four numbered 2 at x = 9.3 and 12.8,
 each pair at y = 0 and 2.5, NPTH 2.4 mm at (6.4, 1.25): the same numbers.
 Mating fuse: ATO / FKS blade fuse (7.5 A and 5 A are standard automotive values).
 The KiCad footprint description says "PCB thickness up to 1.5 mm" for the snap lock; our board is 1.6 mm (see report).
